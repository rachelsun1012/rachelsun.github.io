Meandering river cutoffs

M.Sc. thesis, University of Waterloo

2023 to 2024

ArcGIS Pro, HEC-RAS 2D, R Studio, satellite imagery

I studied how discharge, land cover, and spatial clustering relate to meander cutoffs, where a river abandons a bend and cuts a shorter path. The work combined GIS mapping, hydraulic modelling, and statistics.

  • Processed geospatial, hydrological, and land cover data for 1,186 cutoffs across 12 rivers.
  • Used HEC-RAS 2D for hydraulic modelling and floodplain mapping to analyse channel behaviour and flow dynamics.
  • Tested relationships with ANOVA, Kruskal-Wallis, and Mann-Whitney U, and assessed clustering at multiple scales with Ripley's K.
